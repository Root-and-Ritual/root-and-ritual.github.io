Is the movie phone number still working?

I could not verify a currently working movie or promotional telephone line. No test call was placed, so this page does not advertise a telephone number as active.

Ghostbusters News reported a Sony/Ghost Corps call-and-text campaign in October 2025 using 516-718-TRAP (8727). That historical report does not confirm present availability. It is retained here as promotional history, not a recommended current contact or a call button.

SYFY’s retrospective also reported that the original 1980s promotional hotline was no longer in service when they checked. That old campaign is separate from the 2025 promotion.
