World-Ending Prophecies & Historical Eras

How cultures have imagined the end of the world, alongside real historical eras and present-day risk debates. Some entries are scripture, some are history, and one is archive fiction.

Editorial context: these are unverified project notes, not scholarship. They have not been independently fact-checked, and recent claims follow a single research brief.

Timeline of Endings & Eras

c. 3102 BCE Kali Yuga

The iron age of strife in Hindu cosmology.

Era / date: Ancient Hindu tradition (traditionally said to begin 3102 BCE).

The fourth and final stage of human development in the cycle of yugas, characterized by moral decline, greed, ignorance, and spiritual darkness. According to scripture, it precedes a dissolution before a new cycle begins.

Record status: Religious cosmology

c. 1500–500 BCE Zoroastrian Frashokereti

The renovation of the world in Iranian cosmology.

Era / date: Ancient traditions (origins c. 1500–500 BCE).

In Zoroastrianism, Frashokereti is the final renewal of the universe when evil is completely destroyed and good prevails. It features a final judgment, a metal-melting river, and bodily resurrection, shaping later apocalyptic frameworks in Abrahamic faiths.

Record status: Ancient scripture

Ancient (recorded c. 1220) Ragnarök

The twilight of the gods in Norse mythology.

Era / date: Norse tradition (recorded c. 1200 CE in the Poetic and Prose Eddas).

A sequence of future events including a great winter (Fimbulwinter), epic battles, and natural cataclysms where gods fall and the world is submerged in water, only to be reborn fertile and green.

Record status: Mythological narrative

c. 95 AD Book of Revelation

The Christian apocalypse of John of Patmos.

Era / date: c. 95 AD.

The final book of the New Testament features vivid apocalyptic symbolism: four horsemen, seals, trumpets, the Antichrist, Armageddon, the Millennial reign, and the New Jerusalem. It has fueled end-times expectation for two millennia.

Record status: Canonical scripture

c. 500–1000 The Dark Ages (Early Middle Ages)

The collapse of Western Roman order and cultural transition.

Era / date: c. 500–1000 CE.

A historical period following the fall of Rome marked by demographic shifts, reduced literacy in the West, and fragmented kingdoms. While historians avoid 'Dark Ages' as a value judgment today, it represented an epochal societal collapse and rebirth.

Record status: Historical era

1000 AD 1000 AD Millennialism

Medieval European anxieties around the turn of the millennium.

Era / date: c. 1000 AD.

Popular historical legend holds that Europeans panicked as the year 1000 approached, expecting the Apocalypse. Modern historians note that while apocalyptic thought existed, widespread terror was largely a later exaggeration.

Record status: Historical event / historiographical debate

1844 Millerite Great Disappointment

The failed prediction of the Second Coming by William Miller.

Era / date: October 22, 1844.

Baptist preacher William Miller calculated that Christ would return around 1843–1844 based on biblical prophecy. When the date passed without incident, tens of thousands experienced the 'Great Disappointment', leading to the birth of Seventh-day Adventism.

Record status: Historical movement

1947–2026 Doomsday Clock

The Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists' symbolic countdown.

Era / date: Established 1947; updated annually (90 seconds to midnight in 2026).

Created by Manhattan Project scientists, the clock uses the metaphor of midnight to represent global existential risk from nuclear weapons, climate change, and disruptive technologies. It measures current geopolitical and scientific threat levels.

Record status: Scientific commentary / symbolic metric

1960s Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD)

Cold War nuclear deterrence doctrine.

Era / date: 1960s onward.

A military doctrine holding that a full-scale use of nuclear weapons by two opposing sides would result in the complete annihilation of both the attacker and the defender, enforcing an uneasy peace through the threat of total planetary ruin.

Record status: Strategic military doctrine

2000 Y2K Millennium Bug

The computer software date rollover panic.

Era / date: December 31, 1999 – January 1, 2000.

Computer programs historically stored years using two digits (e.g., '99' for 1999) to save memory, threatening catastrophic failures when clocks rolled over to '00'. Massive global remediation averted widespread disaster, turning a feared apocalypse into a quiet non-event.

Record status: Documented technological milestone

2012 Maya Long Count 2012

The alleged Mesoamerican end-of-the-world phenomenon.

Era / date: December 21, 2012.

The end of the 13th b'ak'tun in the Maya Long Count calendar sparked global media speculation about planetary doom. Archaeologists confirmed the date marked a grand era transition rather than an apocalypse, passing quietly with celebrations.

Record status: Cultural hoax / calendar milestone

Present Planetary Climate Tipping Points

Irreversible ecological disruption thresholds.

Era / date: Ongoing / contemporary scientific consensus.

Earth system science identifies critical thresholds (such as Amazon rainforest dieback, ice sheet collapse, and ocean circulation slowdown) where human-driven warming could trigger self-reinforcing, catastrophic environmental collapse.

Record status: Empirical scientific consensus

Present Global Pathogen & Pandemic Risks

Natural and engineered biological threats.

Era / date: Ongoing.

Public health agencies and biosecurity experts study the risk of catastrophic pandemics, whether zoonotic spillover (like COVID-19 or avian influenza) or accidental/deliberate release of engineered pathogens, capable of collapsing global health and economic systems.

Record status: Public health risk assessment

Deep Time Asteroid Impact

Cosmic impact hazard and extinction events.

Era / date: Geologic deep time / ongoing planetary defense.

The Cretaceous-Paleogene extinction event 66 million years ago proved that a large asteroid impact can wipe out planetary biospheres. Modern space agencies actively track Near-Earth Objects (NEOs) to prevent future cataclysms.

Record status: Geological history / planetary defense

Near future (warnings since 2023) AI Takeover & Superintelligence Fears

Contemporary debates on artificial general intelligence (AGI) risk.

Era / date: 2023–present.

Rapid breakthroughs in generative AI and language models revived serious philosophical and technical warnings regarding existential risk, loss of human control, alignment failure, and societal destabilization caused by autonomous superintelligence.

Record status: Active technological and philosophical debate

Archive Fiction Terminal Epoch

The final closure of the Root & Ritual repository.

Era / date: Unspecified future threshold.

In the cosmology of this archive, the Terminal Epoch represents the theoretical moment when all active links sever, servers go dark, and the accumulated lore reverts entirely back to unwritten earth and quiet digital ash.

Record status: Archive fiction
