VI / Working vocabulary

The language
of the covenant.

Draft lore: this guide develops the supplied fictional setting. New institutions, procedures, and interpretations are proposed worldbuilding, pending author approval.
Accession
The act of accepting an object or record into the archive, with its origin and custody history.
Binding seal
The recorded condition or instrument holding a particular agreement in force. A seal is specific to its record, not a universal symbol.
Canopy treaty
A proposed agreement concerning woodland territory, inheritance, or the continuity of a dynasty.
Celestial jurisdiction
An external claim to authority made under celestial law; its validity may be contested within the setting.
Covenant
An enduring agreement with defined parties, obligations, and conditions. Some covenants precede the firm itself.
Custody chain
The sequence of people or offices responsible for an object or record.
Entity identifier
The stable ENT-number used to link ledger entries and dossiers even when a name changes.
First record
The source named in the supplied entry; it does not necessarily identify the entity’s actual origin.
Ironwood
The setting’s recurring material for durable pacts and artifacts. Its precise physical rules remain open to author development.
Provenance
The documented origin and history of an object or claim.
Sanctorum
The firm’s central archival and administrative refuge.
Soul collateral
An in-world contractual claim involving a person’s metaphysical obligations. Its mechanics are not yet defined in canon.
Threat classification
A proposed measure of potential consequence, distinct from the entity’s current disposition.
Witness
A party whose acknowledged testimony establishes the existence or wording of a pact.
