The Firm · Founding record

The Shared Covenant.

The primal founding pact binding all entity records, wilderness domains, and institutional oversight within the firm.

Draft lore: this guide develops the supplied fictional setting. New institutions, procedures, and interpretations are proposed worldbuilding, pending author approval.

What the Covenant is

The single founding pact between the wilderness and the firm that every entity record is bound under—that is why the Sanctorum shows '01 Shared covenant'. It establishes that raw wilderness cannot be conquered by force alone, and bureaucratic administration cannot survive without deep roots.

Its parties

Entered into by the firm, the sovereign entities of the deep canopy, and the wild places that precede all law. The signatories are both abstract forces and corporate legal persons, bridging ancient territorial sovereignty with modern archival jurisdiction.

The three obligations

First, record faithfully without erasing contradictions or administrative uncertainty. Second, bind entities only by mutual consent or explicit ruling of the Apex Council. Third, keep the boundary between the realms intact, preventing the administrative machinery from leaking into the unmanaged wild.

What happens when it's broken

Breach of the covenant triggers an administrative audit rather than vengeance or summary execution. Records are impounded, disputed accounts are frozen, and auditors from the Audit Halls review the failure of oversight before any corrective binding is applied.

How it touches a case

Every active case filed within the Sanctorum moves strictly through five mandated phases under the covenant: Intake, Identification, Review, Disposition, and Continuity. No claim bypasses this sequence.

Closing line

The ink fades on the parchment, but the root remembers every name ever entered into the ledger.
