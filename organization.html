IV / Institutional guide

The firm beneath
the forest.

A guide to the purpose, structure, and working customs of Root & Ritual.

Draft lore: this guide develops the supplied fictional setting. New institutions, procedures, and interpretations are proposed worldbuilding, pending author approval.

What Root & Ritual does

The firm registers agreements between the inhabited world and the powers that persist beneath it. Its work is not to make the wilderness orderly, but to record where an obligation begins, who accepted it, and what happens when it is broken.

Its authority is contractual rather than universal. A forest, a dynasty, or a relic may recognize one covenant while rejecting another. This makes provenance—the history of a document or object—as consequential as force.

The two authorities

The Root represents territory, inheritance, and the living continuity of a pact. The Ritual represents procedure, witnesses, and the exact terms by which that pact can be invoked. Neither can safely replace the other.

The Apex Council sets the firm’s oldest obligations. The Sanctorum translates those obligations into working records. A council order may be immense in scope while still requiring a properly identified witness.

The Registry

Keeps identifiers, names, classifications, and contract statuses. It records uncertainty rather than resolving a disputed fact by deleting it. Its officers are the first stop for an entity whose identity is unclear.

The Office of Legal Binding

Drafts covenants and reviews claims of authority. The Notary of the Abyss serves as a retained specialist, while the office remains responsible for interpreting exactly what a signature has bound.

The Vault Office

Maintains custody records for secured artifacts and contained entities. Custody describes a location and a chain of responsibility; it does not imply that every occupant is harmless or understood.

The Field Office

Collects observations beyond the Sanctorum. Field officers record conditions, witnesses, and alternative explanations before assigning an encounter to an existing entity.

The Canopy Office

Handles boundaries, woodland infrastructure, and routes between outposts. The Primeval Arborist is consulted on lineage matters; the Moss-Back Behemoth’s structural role requires coordination with the Vault Office.

The Celestial Liaison

Receives claims from external celestial authorities. Its task is to establish jurisdiction before the firm accepts liability, particularly when an auditor demands assets under a competing system of law.

How a case moves through the firm

  1. Intake: record the observation without assigning a conclusion.
  2. Identification: check names, identifiers, and earlier appearances.
  3. Review: establish applicable covenants and disputed authority.
  4. Disposition: document the agreed response and responsible office.
  5. Continuity: retain the prior record when the assessment changes.

Where the archive lives

The Sanctorum is the central administrative refuge. The Audit Halls receive records that cannot be reconciled elsewhere. The vault levels hold access-controlled collections. The perimeter and fen routes connect the firm to the wilderness it can never fully administer.

These are proposed setting roles, not a finalized floor plan. Existing numbered vault references are retained without inventing coordinates or declaring that all references describe the same building.

The Root & Ritual AI

The Root & Ritual AI is the firm’s conversation desk. Ask it about any name, universe or kind of being in the archive, or tell it about something you witnessed and it will file a case with the Intake desk.

What it knows: only the Root & Ritual archive itself: every dossier in the Master Ledger and the firm’s own pages. It cannot see the internet or other websites, and it knows nothing outside the archive. It runs right here in your browser and does not send your questions to any outside AI service.

Search the database and ledger

  1. Open AI Chat (Soon) from the menu and choose 1. Database & ledger search.
  2. Type a name (Gandalf), a universe (Buffyverse, Norse) or a kind of being (vampire, dragon, banshee).
  3. Tap any result to open its dossier. When there are many matches, follow See all in the Master Ledger for the full list.
  4. Questions about the firm itself (the covenant, threat tiers, the glossary) bring up links to the right page.

Submit a case

  1. Choose 2. Submit a case.
  2. Answer one question at a time: a short title, what happened, and optionally where, when, a name or alias, and an email for updates. Tap Skip for anything optional.
  3. Choose I agree — file it. You receive a case number (for example RR-20261022-AB12) and a link to follow the case through Intake, Identification, Review, Disposition and Continuity.

Voice

  • Speak instead of typing: tap the microphone, talk, then tap it again. Your words appear in the message box to check before you send; nothing is sent automatically. Voice input uses your browser’s own speech recognition, which on some browsers is processed by the browser maker’s speech service.
  • Listen to replies: tap Read aloud under any reply. Pause and Stop appear at the top of the chat while it reads.
  • Choose the voice: open Voice (on a phone, Voice & new chat) to pick a reading voice and speed, turn on automatic reading, and set the voice input language. Your choices are remembered on your device.

Privacy: conversations are not saved; refreshing the page or choosing New chat clears them. Only a filed case is kept, as described on the Privacy page.
