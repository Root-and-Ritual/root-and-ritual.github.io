The Sanctorum Manifesto

Wilderness has its laws.
We keep the paperwork.

Root & Ritual is neither mere corporation nor secret coven—it is an enduring geological and metaphysical architecture. We operate at the intersection of primordial wildness and absolute legalistic machinery. Here, pacts are carved into petrified ironwood, and every decree is bound by ancient operational law.

Operatives navigating this domain understand that survival requires both the sovereign fury of the forest and the impenetrable armor of procedural ritual.
